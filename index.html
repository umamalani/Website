KinetiCare

A browser-based AI physical therapy coach: your webcam watches your form and a coach talks you through it.

33body landmarks tracked at 30 fps, on-device
5voice coach personas
<5 minfrom intake to post-session report
webcam ──▶ MediaPipe Pose   33 landmarks @ 30 fps, in the browser
                │
                ▼   every 8 s, if the coach isn't already talking
          Gemini 2.5 Flash  reads joint angles vs. your calibration,
                │            fatigue, expression, one frame
                ▼
          ElevenLabs        speaks one targeted correction
  • A planning agent turns the user's injury and goals into an exercise protocol, and three calibration poses capture their own joint angles, so form is scored against their body instead of generic thresholds.
  • The hard part was concurrency: pose detection, model calls, speech and React state all running at once. Async queueing and voice-busy detection keep the coach from talking over itself.
  • Feedback was first sent every frame, which was unbearable. Tuning it to every 8 seconds made the coach useful.
  • A report agent writes a PT-style note with range-of-motion data and flags for when to see a real therapist.
